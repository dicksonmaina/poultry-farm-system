# Business Model

## Open Source Core

The core system remains free and open source under MIT license. This builds trust, community, and real-world usage.

## Revenue Streams

1. **Hosted Cloud Version**
   - Managed hosting with automatic updates
   - Pricing: $5–10 per farm per month
   - Includes backups, support, and uptime SLA

2. **Customization & Integration**
   - Tailored modules for specific farm operations
   - Integration with existing accounting or ERP systems
   - Typical engagements: $100–500 per project

3. **Installation & Training**
   - Full setup for new farms
   - Staff training and documentation
   - Pricing: $50–200 per engagement

4. **Support Subscriptions**
   - Priority support for active deployments
   - Pricing: $20–50 per month per client

5. **Partnerships & Referrals**
   - Integration with feed suppliers, veterinary services, insurance
   - Commission-based referrals from within the system

## Target Market

- Small to medium poultry farms in Kenya and East Africa
- Agricultural cooperatives
- Agritech startups and students
- NGOs working in food security and livestock

## Go-to-Market Strategy

1. Build community via GitHub stars and contributions
2. Recruit 5–10 pilot farms for free deployment
3. Collect testimonials and case studies
4. Launch cloud version with freemium tier
5. Partner with agriculture input suppliers
