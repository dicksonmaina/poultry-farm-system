# Pilot Farm Recruitment Package

## Goal
Recruit 5–10 pilot farms in Kenya/East Africa for free 12-month poultry-farm-system deployment in exchange for testimonials, bug reports, and case-study data.

## Why This Is the Next Revenue Action
- Pilot farms validate product-market fit before paid launch
- Testimonials from real farmers convert cold leads better than any marketing copy
- Bug reports from real usage harden the product faster than internal QA
- Each pilot farm is a potential long-term paying customer and referral source

## Target Farm Profile
- 500–5,000 birds per cycle
- Currently using spreadsheets, WhatsApp, or paper records
- Located in Kenya (primary), Uganda/Tanzania (secondary)
- Has basic smartphone access for web app usage
- Owner or manager can make software purchase decisions
- Willing to provide weekly feedback and a final testimonial

## Outreach Channels — Ranked by Conversion Probability

### 1. Direct WhatsApp/Telegram to Known Farms (Highest Conversion)
- Source: existing Telegram groups, agrovet contacts, farmer cooperatives
- Message: short, value-first pitch with 2-min demo video
- Offer: free setup + 12 months free + priority support
- Ask: weekly 10-min feedback call
- Target: 20 personal outreaches → expect 3–5 conversions

### 2. Agricultural Cooperatives (Medium Conversion, Scale)
- Kenya Poultry Farmers Association
- Local cooperatives in Kiambu, Nakuru, Kericho
- Pitch to cooperative leadership: bulk license for members
- Offer: cooperative gets branded instance + admin panel
- Target: 5 cooperative meetings → expect 2–3 pilots

### 3. Agrovet Shops (Referral Partners)
- Agrovets sell to 20–50 farms each
- Offer agrovet: referral fee or free instance for their farm clients
- Agrovet benefits: differentiated service offering
- Target: 10 agrovet partnerships → expect 2–4 referrals

### 4. Social Media (Lowest Conversion, Broad Reach)
- Facebook poultry farming groups
- Instagram agriculture accounts
- TikTok farmer content creators
- Content: before/after spreadsheet → web app demo
- Target: consistent posting for 4 weeks → expect 1–2 inbound

### 5. Reddit/Online Communities (Lowest Conversion, Long Tail)
- r/kenya, r/AgriculturalTechnology, r/selfhosted
- Post: open-source tool + pilot program announcement
- Offer: free setup for first 10 farms globally
- Target: 1–2 international pilot farms for credibility

## Outreach Message Templates

### WhatsApp/Telegram Direct Message
```
Hi {Name}, I'm building a poultry farm management system for Kenyan farmers. It tracks birds, feed, eggs, sales, and expenses — everything you currently do in spreadsheets or notebooks.

I'm looking for 10 pilot farms to test it for free for 12 months. In exchange, I need 10 minutes of your time each week for feedback and a short testimonial at the end.

No cost. No commitment. If it's not useful, you walk away.

Would you be open to a quick call or demo this week?
```

### Cooperative Presentation Script
```
Good morning. I'm here to show you how poultry farmers in your cooperative can save 5–10 hours per week on record-keeping.

Currently, most farmers track their birds, feed, and sales in notebooks or WhatsApp. This creates three problems: lost records, no analytics, and no proof for loans or buyers.

Our system digitizes all of that — bird inventory, feed schedules, egg production, sales, expenses — and generates reports you can use for M-Pesa statements, SACCO applications, and buyer negotiations.

We're offering the first 10 farms 12 months free. All we ask is weekly feedback.

I'll leave you with a one-page summary. Let me know who wants to try it first.
```

### Agrovet Referral Pitch
```
Hi {Name}, your farm clients are asking for better record-keeping. We built a poultry management system that runs in the browser — no app download, works on any smartphone.

I'd like to offer your top 5 clients a free 12-month trial. You get to offer them a premium service without any cost to you. For every client who upgrades to paid, you get a referral bonus.

Can I send you a 2-minute demo video to share with your clients?
```

## Pilot Onboarding Workflow
1. Sign-up form submitted → auto-create tenant in SaaS instance
2. Admin receives notification → schedule 30-min onboarding call within 48 hours
3. Onboarding call: walk through dashboard, enter current flock data, set up reminders
4. Week 1 check-in: 15-min call to fix issues and collect first impression
5. Weekly check-ins: 10-min calls for first month, then bi-weekly
6. Month 3: request mid-program testimonial and case study data
7. Month 6: review upgrade options, offer 50% discount for first year
8. Month 12: final testimonial, case study publication, upgrade conversation

## Success Metrics
- Enrollment: 5–10 farms within 30 days
- Retention: 8+ farms active at month 6
- Testimonials: 3+ video or written testimonials by month 6
- Case studies: 2+ detailed case studies by month 12
- Revenue: 3+ farms convert to paid by month 12
- Referrals: 2+ new farms referred by existing pilots by month 12

## What Pilot Farms Get Free
- Full SaaS access (normally $5–10/farm/month)
- Priority support via WhatsApp/Telegram
- Custom feature requests prioritized
- Free onboarding and data migration
- Certificate of participation for SACCO/loan applications

## What We Get From Pilots
- Weekly 10-min feedback calls
- Bug reports and feature requests
- Monthly usage data and screenshots
- Final testimonial (video preferred)
- Permission to use farm name/logo in marketing
- Case study data for blog/social media

## Pilot Farm Agreement
- 12-month free trial, no credit card required
- Either party can cancel anytime with 7 days notice
- Feedback is voluntary but strongly encouraged
- Testimonial/content usage requires explicit written consent
- Data remains property of the farm; we only use anonymized data for product improvement
