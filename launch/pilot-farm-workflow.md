# Pilot Farm Recruitment Workflow

## Goal
Recruit 5–10 pilot farms for free deployment in exchange for testimonials, bug reports, and case study material.

## Ideal Pilot Profile
- 500–3,000 birds
- Currently using spreadsheets, paper, or WhatsApp
- Located in Kenya or East Africa
- Willing to try new software and provide feedback
- Has basic smartphone or computer access
- Owner or manager can make purchase decisions

## Outreach Channels
1. **Telegram agriculture groups** — direct outreach to farm owners
2. **WhatsApp groups** — Kenya poultry farmer communities
3. **Facebook groups** — Poultry Farming Kenya, Kienyeji Chicken Farmers
4. **Instagram** — visual testimonials from farm visits
5. **Local cooperatives** — agricultural cooperative networks
6. **Agrovet connections** — feed and vaccine suppliers who serve multiple farms

## Pitch
- Free full access to Poultry Manager Cloud for 3 months
- Free onboarding and setup
- Direct support via WhatsApp/phone
- In exchange: weekly feedback, testimonial, case study photos/video

## Onboarding Steps
1. Sign up via free tier or direct pilot invite
2. Migrate current data: flock count, production records, expenses
3. Configure farm settings: bird types, housing, staff roles
4. Train owner/manager: 30-minute video call or WhatsApp walkthrough
5. Daily check-in for first week, then weekly
6. Collect feedback and fix issues
7. Capture before/after metrics for case study

## Success Metrics
- 5 farms onboarded within 30 days
- 80%+ weekly active usage after week 2
- 3+ video testimonials
- 2+ written case studies
- 10+ GitHub stars from pilot network
- 5+ referrals to other farms

## Compensation for Pilots
- Free 12-month subscription after pilot period
- Priority support for 1 year
- Free custom module development worth $200–500
- Featured on website and marketing materials
- Early access to new features

## Timeline
- Week 1: Outreach and signups
- Week 2: Onboarding first 2 farms
- Week 3: Onboarding next 3 farms
- Week 4: Collect initial testimonials and iterate
