# Telegram Group Operations — JARVIS Managed Group

## Purpose
Operational model for the Telegram group JARVIS will eventually own and manage on Richard's behalf. Group management is treated as a revenue and community asset, not an experiment.

## Ownership Model
- Richard remains the owner
- JARVIS operates as the group manager/admin assistant
- All group activity is aligned to poultry-farm-system revenue goals
- The group is a lead-generation, support, and distribution channel

## Required Bot Permissions
Before management can begin, `@Rixikibot` must be added to the target group and promoted to admin with:
- Send messages
- Send media
- Pin messages
- Delete messages
- Add users
- Manage topics

Without these, management is limited to observation and basic interaction.

## Startup Rules
1. Richard creates/provisions the group
2. Richard adds `@Rixikibot` as admin
3. Richard shares the group ID with JARVIS
4. JARVIS initializes group config and automation
5. JARVIS begins welcome and lead capture flows
6. JARVIS reports weekly group metrics to Richard

## Group Safety Policy
- No public sharing of sensitive credentials
- No member PII beyond what is needed for support
- Spam and abuse handled by bot first, escalated to Richard only when needed
- Clear community rules posted and enforced consistently
- Admin actions logged for review

## Admin Escalation Path
- JARVIS handles routine moderation and automation
- Complex disputes, refunds, or legal issues escalate to Richard
- Weekly summary of flagged items, new leads, and support volume sent to Richard
- Emergency shutdown available if behavior degrades

## Revenue Integration
- Support requests from the group flow into `pages/support.php`
- Leads are tagged by source for conversion tracking
- Group activity metrics feed into pilot farm recruitment
- Community members get early access to hosted SaaS features
- Potential paid community tier or exclusive member benefits

## Success Metrics
- 50+ active members within 30 days
- 10+ support requests or trial signups per month
- 80%+ message response rate from JARVIS
- 3+ pilot farms recruited from the group per month
