# Pilot Farm Recruitment — Poultry Farm Management System

## Goal
Recruit 5-10 pilot farms for free deployment in exchange for:
- Testimonials after 30-60 days
- Bug reports and feature requests
- Case study content for marketing
- Referrals to other farmers

## Target Profile
- Small to medium poultry farms in Kenya/East Africa
- 500-5,000 birds
- Currently using spreadsheets or paper records
- Owner/manager active on WhatsApp or Telegram
- Willing to spend 1-2 hours on onboarding

## Outreach Channels
- Local poultry farming groups on WhatsApp/Telegram
- Kenya Poultry Farmers Association
- Agricultural cooperatives
- Agritech startup networks
- Direct visits to farms in Kiambu, Nakuru, Eldoret

## Pitch Template

> Hi [Name],
>
> I’m Richard, a developer and poultry farmer from Kenya. I built a free farm management system to help small farms move from spreadsheets to a proper system.
>
> I’m looking for 5-10 pilot farms to test it for free. In exchange, I just need your feedback after a month — what works, what doesn’t, and what you’d pay for.
>
> No cost. No commitment. Just honest feedback.
>
> Would you be interested?

## Onboarding Flow
1. Deploy on their server or my hosted instance
2. 30-minute training call
3. Import existing flock data
4. Daily check-ins for first week
5. Weekly feedback call for first month
6. Testimonial + case study at 60 days

## Success Metrics
- 5+ farms actively using after 30 days
- 3+ testimonials published
- 2+ feature requests prioritized
- 1+ referral to another farm
